# My Candidate Profile

| **Product Manager (Platform/Dev Tools)** | Technical depth, developer empathy, API product thinking | Secondary |
| **Head of Product** | Team leadership, strategy, exec stakeholder management | Stretch |

## Deal-Breakers (auto-dismiss if any apply)
- Role is 100% on-site with no remote flexibility
- Compensation is commission-only or equity-only
- Role is clearly a contractor/freelance engagement

## Soft Flags (reduce score by 1-2 points)
- Company has no public funding or revenue signal
- Job description is under 200 words (low signal)
- Role requires >50% travel

## Scoring Notes
- Strongly prefer companies with: strong engineering culture, clear product ownership, data-informed decisions
- Bonus for: remote-first, async culture, funded Series B+, product-led growth
- Negative signal: sales-driven culture forcing PM to be a PM-BA hybrid
